// Where To Show Up

Be In The Room.

Public comment is where contracts get killed. We want someone from DeFlock Mesa County at as many of these as possible — council meetings, agenda/workshop sessions, all of it — even if all that happens is one person standing up during open comment to say we're here and we want the cameras gone. Showing up consistently is what makes an elected body start taking a campaign seriously.

Grand Junction City Council
FAQs

GJPD holds the city's ALPR contract. Regular council meetings and workshop/agenda sessions both take public comment — the agenda sessions get less attention and fewer people show up, which makes a voice there count for more.

// If You Can't Show Up

Email The People
Who Signed For It.

Showing up to a meeting is still the most effective thing you can do. But a clear, specific email from a constituent is a real action too, and it takes about two minutes.

Pick your jurisdiction, choose who should hear from you, and this builds the message. Nothing is sent from this site and no address is collected — the draft opens in your own email app so it arrives from you.

01 / PICK
Choose your city or the county, then tap the officials who should hear from you.
02 / WRITE
Start from a campaign template and edit it. A message in your own words counts for far more than a form letter.
03 / SEND
Open it in your email app and send. Or copy the recipients, subject, and body by hand.
