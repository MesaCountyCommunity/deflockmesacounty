Mesa County, CO DeFlock Mesa County

Your Privacy is
Being Violated

Mesa County has a network of over 30 automated license plate readers (ALPRs). These AI-powered cameras are being used to build a detailed profile on everyone: without a warrant, without public oversight, and with your tax dollars. Your information is then plugged into a nationwide mass surveillance system.

DeFlock Mesa County is a grassroots organization working to remove AI-driven mass surveillance systems from our community and secure legal privacy protections. Stand with us to take action.

// What We Are Working Toward

DeFlock Mesa County's Goals

1
End the ALPR Contracts

It’s not just Flock; the GJPD and the Mesa County Sheriff’s Office have also contracted with other ALPR providers, such as Motorola Solutions. We want all contracts canceled and their cameras removed.
2
Pass a CCOPS Ordinance

Ending a contract with Flock is only part of the solution– we need to protect our privacy in perpetuity against future surveillance technology. Passing a Community Control Over Police Surveillance (CCOPS) ordinance would mean that no surveillance technology could be acquired without a public hearing; no surveillance data could be accessed without a warrant; and that the use of surveillance data by the police would be subject to local civilian oversight.

Community Control Over Police Surveillance (CCOPS) means no surveillance technology is acquired or deployed until the elected body votes on it in a public hearing; every system has a written use policy covering who may access the data, for what purposes, and how long it is kept; and the department publishes an annual report on how each technology was actually used and every outside agency the data went to.

3
Ban Facial Recognition Outright

We want a full prohibition on the use of any facial recognition technology. Facial recognition technology is invasive, unreliable, and a threat to our constitutional rights and safety.
